Know the four categories.

The first step towards responsible waste management is understanding what you are disposing of.

🍃
01 / ORGANIC

Biodegradable

Organic material that can break down naturally under suitable conditions.

Fruit peels · Food scraps · Leaves

♻️
02 / DRY MATERIAL

Recyclable

Paper · Cardboard · Glass
